Good questions first

team up.

What a brand partner usually wants to know before starting a conversation.

What is ApexGaming.fp?

ApexGaming.fp is an independent Instagram fan page focused on BGMI esports updates, tournament moments, gaming highlights and community-led content. It is not an official esports organization or game publisher account.

Which brands are a good fit?

Gaming accessories, mobile and performance products, gaming apps, esports events, digital services and creator tools can be a fit when the offer is relevant to the audience. Every brief is reviewed before acceptance.

What does the audience snapshot show?

The supplied Instagram Insights audience screenshot shows 96.1% of the audience in India. The profile dashboard screenshot shows 9.3 lakh views in the last 30 days. Individual screenshots may represent different reporting windows.

Which campaign formats can brands book?

Potential formats include Instagram Stories, feed posts, Reels and bundled campaigns. Final deliverables depend on the campaign brief, publishing schedule and mutual agreement.

Can you share analytics with a sponsor?

Selected Insights screenshots are shown above. For a relevant campaign discussion, additional available screenshots can be shared to help align on objectives and reporting expectations.

How much does a promotion cost?

Pricing depends on the format, production requirements, timeline, usage rights and exclusivity. Submit a campaign brief to receive a tailored proposal rather than a generic package.

Can I request a call instead of emailing back and forth?

Yes. Choose “Request a Call” in the form below, add a business contact number and a convenient callback time. Your number is sent with the inquiry and is not displayed publicly on this website.
